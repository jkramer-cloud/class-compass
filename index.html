4. Backups and portability

Important: Without an account, this planner cannot automatically follow you to another Chromebook. A managed Chromebook may also clear browser data at sign-out. Save your backup file regularly.

The JSON backup includes student name, archived years, class schedules, assignments, progress checklists, custom calendar events, and theme settings. No data is uploaded by the app.

5. School calendar

Naches Valley School District's 2026–27 and 2027–28 calendar dates are included. Students can also add their own school events, appointments, or deadline reminders.

The 2027–28 calendar has mismatched text and color blocks: its spring-break text says April 5–9, but its April 2028 grid highlights April 3–7. The planner shows April 3–7 provisionally; confirm with the school. Winter-break text and highlighting also differ by one day.
